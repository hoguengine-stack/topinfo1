## Hard Gates

### Fact And Product Fidelity

- [ ] Exact manufacturer/model/variant is documented.
- [ ] Product geometry and physical proportions match official specifications.
- [ ] Official UI is current, readable, not generated, and not cropped incorrectly.
- [ ] Platform/provider responsibility and TOPINFO responsibility are separated.
- [ ] Price, promotion, compatibility, and service claims have current conditions and sources.
- [ ] Partner/logo/media redistribution and editing rights are documented.

### Image Craft

- [ ] No white matte box, halo, spill, watermark, blur, or low-resolution enlargement.
- [ ] Full silhouette is visible unless the role is an intentional detail crop.
- [ ] Products share coherent perspective, ground plane, light, color, and shadow.
- [ ] Products do not intersect, float, or disappear behind other devices.
- [ ] Relative scale is calculated from physical dimensions.
- [ ] The image has a clear focal hierarchy and balanced negative space.
- [ ] Repeated assets have distinct editorial roles.
- [ ] Product/store/people media cannot be mistaken for an unverified real case.

### Typography And Layout

- [ ] Korean uses horizontal writing with no direct or inherited rotation.
- [ ] No one-character wrapping, clipped labels, or forced breaks that fail on mobile.
- [ ] Critical body is at least 14px and mobile fields are at least 16px.
- [ ] Header, content, media, and footer share intentional guides.
- [ ] 1560px frame and 1320px content contract is measured at 2560px.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow or off-screen interactive control.
- [ ] Fixed UI does not cover content.

### Design Quality

- [ ] Content and evidence lead decoration.
- [ ] No generic AI gradient/glass/blob/floating-device pattern.
- [ ] Sections do not repeat the same card template.
- [ ] TOPINFO remains the page owner; Toss blue stays in Toss context.
- [ ] One claim, one dominant visual, and one primary CTA per major section.
- [ ] Text volume, hierarchy, and whitespace support fast scanning.
- [ ] Motion demonstrates a real process and respects reduced motion.

### Accessibility And Interaction

- [ ] Semantic link/button/menu/tab/accordion roles are correct.
- [ ] Keyboard, Escape, focus entry/return, and focus visibility work.
- [ ] Touch targets are at least 44px where practical.
- [ ] Meaningful images have alt; decoration is hidden from assistive tech.
- [ ] Form labels, errors, loading, failure, and actual success are connected.
- [ ] Color contrast meets WCAG AA and color is not the only state cue.
