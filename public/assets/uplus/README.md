The source images are shown without altering the LG U+ logo or product proportions. Product media uses `object-fit: contain`; the public UI must not crop equipment or instructional content to fill a card.

`uplus-ip520ga-white.png` is the official 214×150 transparent product image published on LG U+'s enterprise internet-phone page. It is intentionally rendered at a controlled size rather than being regenerated as a larger fake product. The retired `uplus-ip520ga-hero.png` derivative was moved to `.asset-research/rights-pending/public-blocked/uplus/` and is blocked from public re-entry.

Before production publication, confirm that the direct-agency asset terms permit local redistribution and replace these files with partner-provided originals when available.

The former `uplus-ai-phone-store.jpg` newsroom portrait is intentionally retired. The source identifies the person as an LG U+ employee, and a public press image does not by itself document third-party advertising or portrait-use permission. AI phone sections now use an original code-rendered service graphic without an identifiable person.

`uplus-package-overview.png` and `uplus-ai-phone-ip520g.jpg` were removed from `public/` and archived under `.asset-research/rights-pending/public-blocked/uplus/`. They are blocked as an outdated package composite and an older model/reference image respectively.
