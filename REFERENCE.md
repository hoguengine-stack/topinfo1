# TOPINFO Reference Analysis

Updated: 2026-07-18

This file records reusable design and sales principles. It is not permission to copy text, images, product UI, illustrations, logos, or complete layouts. TOPINFO business facts and documented media rights always take priority.

## Chosen Art Direction

**Store handover record:** TOPINFO shows how a store moves from operating need to verified equipment, installation, education, and support. Real store context and real product evidence lead; decoration follows.

The site should not resemble a collage of competitors. Each reference has one role:

| Reference | Useful role | TOPINFO transformation | Do not use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Toss Place | Short copy paired with large verified hardware/UI | Add TOPINFO configuration, installation, education, and support context | Toss page composition, copy voice, blue brand takeover, unlicensed UI/media |
| Torder | Large usage scenes and story rhythm | Follow a store's operating day with restrained transitions | Automatic visual spectacle that hides product facts |
| POSMOS | Package conditions, price context, process, FAQ | Use once in a maintained promotion module with all conditions visible | `무료` as the hero, partner brands as the site owner |
| iShopCare | Owner-first navigation by situation and industry | Cross-filter opening, replacement, network, and support needs | Shopping-mall card walls and ranking theatrics |
| Sinsin MNC | Installation evidence and strong consultation path | Show TOPINFO's own cases, responsibility, area, and support route | Competitor metrics, claims, photographs, popups, repeated CTA blocks |
| Hielpay | Product family and form classification | Reverse the sequence: owner problem -> form -> verified model | Image-only promotions and copied catalogue text |
| SHPOS | Manuals, consumables, and practical support | Build a support hub for AS, paper, resources, and suggestions | Shopping cart/login-first information architecture |

Typography review, checked 2026-07-19:

- The useful principle is a small set of repeated Korean text roles rather than a different size for every section.
- Body copy keeps a relaxed line height and controlled measure; buttons and short actions remain on one line.
- Korean copy uses word-preserving wrapping, while telephone and action tokens are explicitly non-wrapping.
- TOPINFO adopts these readability rules with Pretendard and its own fixed breakpoint scale. It does not copy iShopCare copy, component styling, metrics, or page composition.

## Reference Principles To Adopt

1. Let an owner enter through a store problem, industry, or support task.
2. Show real hardware and official UI large enough to inspect.
3. Use one claim and one conversion action per major section.
4. Explain product combinations through required/optional/conditional status.
5. Separate platform features from TOPINFO installation/support responsibilities.
6. Show complete promotion conditions in HTML, not baked into a campaign image.
7. Separate new customer consultation from existing customer support.
8. Use actual installation evidence once publication rights exist.
9. Keep industry pages organized by operating sequence.
10. Use motion to demonstrate a process, never to hide missing evidence.

## Deliberately Rejected Patterns

- Hero claims centered on `무료`, `0원`, or a giant unqualified price
- Partner-logo walls that make TOPINFO look like a reseller with no responsibility
- Repeated feature-card grids with generic line icons
- Generic blue/purple SaaS or glassmorphism
- Floating AI-generated hardware and unreadable generated Korean UI
- Automatic tab rotation that overrides the owner's selection
- Campaign conditions hidden in 10px footnotes or a single image
- Made-up installations, reviews, staff, people, or performance results
- Copying the visible composition of any one competitor

## Rights And Evidence Notes

- Site access does not grant reuse rights.
- Product renders, UI screenshots, partner logos, customer photos, testimonials, certification marks, prices, and benefit claims are high-risk assets.
- Maintain official URL, capture date, local source hash, allowed edits, page usage, and next review date.
- Use locally created non-product backgrounds only when they cannot be mistaken for an actual installation case.
- Before production publication, replace public web copies with partner-provided masters where available and confirm agency redistribution rights.
